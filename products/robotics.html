Robotics

Our robotics solutions excel in challenging conditions, from Nuclear Decontamination and CBRNE scenarios to VR-enabled remote operations and NDT. Designed for maximum safety, precision, and efficiency, they transform hazardous tasks into safe, accurate operations, minimizing human risk.

MiniROV – EOD Robot

A compact, battery-operated land-based EOD robotic system for safe detection, identification, and neutralization of hazardous objects, with robust mobility, modular payload integration, and dual communication modes.

Defence
  • Overall dimensions 1600 × 1200 × 1200 mm; maximum weight up to 100 kg
  • Rechargeable battery, minimum 2 hours continuous operation
  • Remote range ≥250 m (radio) and ≥50 m (fibre optic)
  • Climbs slopes and stairs up to 45°
  • Minimum lifting capacity 8 kg; telescopic arm extends up to 2 m
  • Compact, portable operator control station
  • Front and rear cameras for real-time situational awareness
  • Modular mounts for disruptor, RTVS, lifting bracket and shotgun
  • Operating temperature –15°C to +55°C
02 / 06

VIGILBOT – AI Security & Surveillance Robot

An AI-powered robot for autonomous patrolling and real-time threat detection at forward bases, ammunition depots and LAC posts, with continuous perimeter monitoring day and night.

Defence
  • Autonomous patrolling with real-time threat detection and alerts
  • AI computer vision for day and night
  • Detects and classifies humans, animals, vehicles and weapons
  • Intelligent alarms for unauthorized movement and breaches
  • User-friendly dashboard for monitoring and control

CBRNE Robots (Nuclear Decontamination)

Engineered for cleaning and decontaminating highly radioactive areas within hot cells, with sophisticated decontamination technology and an in-house Pan-Tilt Camera. Robots specially designed and commissioned for the Department of Atomic Energy, Government of India.

NuclearPublic SafetyInfrastructure

Advanced Navigation & Control

In-house Pan-Tilt camera for safe navigation in radiation zones; collision-free operation with proximity sensors; remote control from safe distances.

Superior Decontamination

A variety of cleaning tools including abrasive scrubbers and chemical applicators.

Enhanced Safety

Radiation-resistant materials for durability and reliability.

Modular & Customizable

Interchangeable modules; customizable to any nuclear facility and task size.

Decontamination of floor, tools & equipment inside a hot cellDecontamination of rescue aircraft and their cockpit control electronicsPortable decontamination spray/bath for operators after work in radiation-prone zones

VR Controlled Robots

A remote inspection and operation system with a 6 DOF collaborative robot, a precision 2-finger gripper and a radiation sensor, for nuclear energy, medical research and high-tech manufacturing. Designed, commissioned and tested for Government of India research organizations.

DefenceNuclearOil & GasPublic SafetyEducationIndustry

6 DOF COBOT

Mimics human arm movements with high precision in tight spaces.

2-Finger Gripper

Handles everything from delicate components to robust tools.

HD 3D Camera

Detailed views via VR for remote inspection and operation.

Versatile Use

Remote inspections, operating panels (MCBs, push buttons, toggle switches) and EOD operations.

Remote inspection and operation through telepresence in pipelines, industrial facilities or disaster zonesVR-based training and education for industries, excellence centres and collegesShowcasing capabilities at exhibitions and trade shows
